# R01 transfer brief

## Visual purpose

Make the current operational stage understandable without giving every datum equal weight; make an intervention’s consequences visible before or as it propagates.

## Transferable principles

- Give the ordered work sequence compositional priority.
- Layer secondary evidence behind the active stage.
- Preserve manual intervention and make downstream effect explicit.
- Use calm density, not decorative scarcity.

## Signatures to avoid

- Aviation colors, runway imagery, or a literal departure line-up.
- The reference’s exact composition, styling, icons, copy, or timing.
- Reusing archive-reported environmental or operational claims as project proof.

## Original adaptation boundary

Railway Control Plane may express its own five-stage workflow rail—requested, validated, dispatched, observed, settled—only when each stage maps to actual product state. No visual geometry is prescribed by this pack.
