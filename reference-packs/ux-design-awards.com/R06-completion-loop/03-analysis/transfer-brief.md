# R06 transfer brief

## Visual purpose

Turn failure from a dead end into an evidence-backed recovery path, and withhold “done” until observation verifies the result.

## Transferable principles

- Begin recovery with the anomaly and supporting evidence.
- Prioritize one highest-value next action.
- Distinguish action dispatch from observed completion.
- Preserve the verification evidence in the ledger.

## Signatures to avoid

- Geospatial leak maps, acoustic/ML decoration, or utility-sector visual language.
- Exact confirmation affordances, composition, copy, or interaction behavior.
- Premature green success or hidden retry attempts.
